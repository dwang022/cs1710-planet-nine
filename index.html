04 — The search

Where we have actually looked

Flattened map of the entire sky. Shaded patches are where big surveys have hunted. The green curve is where Planet Nine would have to be.

The pale band is the Milky Way. Star fields there are so crowded that a slow-moving object is very hard to pick out — and the green path crosses it twice. Footprints are simplified to declination bands: good enough to see who looked where, not for precise coverage. Planet Nine's path is computed from the Brown & Batygin (2021) orbit fit; thin lines show their uncertainty.
