A planet is not the only explanation

The pattern needs explaining even if Planet Nine does not exist. Here are eight ideas, from a planet to no pattern at all, scored against the four things any of them must account for, followed by the papers that have put the case against a planet.

Eight explanations, four tests

Reading across a row tells you where each idea breaks. Click any row to see what it explains well and where its critics say it falls short.

The assessments are our summary of the published arguments, not a quantitative ranking. "Nothing has been found" counts against a planet because a decade of searching has turned up none; it counts for every idea that needs no planet.

So far

Two explanations have survived every test so far: a hidden planet, and a pattern made by the telescopes that found it. The others each fail at least one. The next page lays out the whole argument, year by year.
