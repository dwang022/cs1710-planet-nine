The case against

The studies arguing that the pattern is an artefact of where telescopes looked, that something other than a planet produces it, or that a new discovery does not fit.

Which side a paper lands on is our reading of its abstract, not a label the authors chose. The drawings are schematic. The full ledger, both sides, is on the next page.
