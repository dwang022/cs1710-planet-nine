Three more discoveries

  1. 1930

    Clyde Tombaugh finds Pluto by flipping between two plates taken six days apart.

  2. 2003

    Astronomers find Sedna, a likely dwarf planet that never comes within — of the Sun.

  3. 2015

    A camera built to study dark energy finds 2015 BP519, one of the most extreme orbits known.

JPL's catalogue

NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) keeps the orbit of every known body in the solar system.
