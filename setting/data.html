JPL's catalogue

NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) keeps the orbit of every known body in the solar system.

What makes an orbit reliable

Every year an object is seen again, its orbit is known better. Like earlier studies2,3, we count only objects seen in more than one year.

Why count years, not uncertainty? A few months of sightings can fit many orbits, even when the uncertainty looks small. A second year makes the orbit more reliable.
