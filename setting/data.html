Thousands of bodies, a few dozen that count

NASA's JPL and the Minor Planet Center keep the orbit of every known body in the solar system.

Years of watching pin down an orbit

Whether an object makes the cut depends on telescopes finding it again, year after year.

Every position from another season shrinks the uncertainty in its orbit. Like the studies of Planet Nine1,2, this site counts only objects seen in more than one season.
